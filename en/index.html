About the Workshop

Rare disease diagnosis increasingly requires the integration of genomic information, structured clinical phenotypes, and medical imaging.

This workshop brings together German and Japanese researchers working on next-generation phenotyping, including the Human Phenotype Ontology (HPO), GA4GH Phenopackets, GestaltMatcher, PEDIA, PubCaseFinder, multimodal phenotyping, genomic diagnostics, and computational facial phenotyping.

The collaboration addresses three central challenges:

  • Privacy and sensitivity
  • Standardization and interoperability
  • Fairness across populations

The longer-term goal is to develop fair, privacy-preserving, multimodal approaches that integrate structured phenotype information, imaging, and genomic data for rare disease diagnosis.

Collaboration

Workshop Goals

  • Establish a Japan–Germany collaboration on next-generation phenotyping.
  • Harmonize HPO, genomic, and imaging data.
  • Explore integration with GA4GH Phenopackets.
  • Discuss privacy-preserving and hospital-hosted approaches.
  • Study ancestry-related differences and fairness across populations.
  • Develop plans for joint studies and future international collaboration.
